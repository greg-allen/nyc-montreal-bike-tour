📍 8 Crucial Navigation Checkpoints: Do Not Miss!
1. Manhattan Exit: Dyckman St → Broadway Bridge → Van Cortlandt Park connection to South County Trailway.
2. Brewster Transition: Exit Putnam Trailway → cross Route 6 directly onto Maybrook Trailway.
3. Walkway Over Hudson: Dutchess Rail Trail connects directly onto the pedestrian bridge deck (Highland).
4. Albany AHET Entry: Follow Route 9G north through Rhinebeck into Hudson Warren St, pick up AHET north to Rensselaer.
5. Waterford Lock 2: Cross Mohawk River on Route 32 bridge → Champlain Canalway Trail north.
6. Lake Champlain Bluffs: Follow Route 22/9N rolling hills through Port Henry & Essex (DO NOT speed on wet descents).
7. Lacolle Border (Rte 221): Cyclists ride up to vehicle booth. Have physical passport in hand. ZERO produce or THC!
8. Montréal Grand Entry: Chambly Canal path → Voie Maritime → Samuel De Champlain Bridge bike path → Cité du Havre → Old Port!
