# NYC to Montréal Multi-Day Bicycle Tour Guide (Sunday to Friday • 6 Days)
**A Leisurely 6-Day Tour from New York City to Montréal via the Empire State Trail, Lake Champlain Coast & Route Verte**

*Designed for Google Docs copy-pasting, web visualization, and offline cue sheet printing.*

---

## Executive Summary & Trip Overview

- **Schedule:** **Depart Sunday Morning (NYC) &rarr; Arrive Friday Afternoon (Montréal)**
  - Leaves the entire Friday evening, Saturday, and Sunday open for enjoying Montréal before returning via Amtrak or car rental.
- **Total Distance:** ~389 miles (626 km)
- **Daily Mileage Average:** ~64.8 miles (104 km) per day
- **Pace:** Steady Leisurely Touring (12–14 mph rolling speed, approx. 4.75–5.5 hours rolling time per day)
- **Surface Profile:**
  - **Days 1–3 (NYC to Saratoga Springs):** ~75% paved traffic-free rail trails (Hudson River Greenway, South/North County Trailway, Maybrook Trailway, Dutchess Rail Trail, Albany-Hudson Electric Trail, Zim Smith Trail).
  - **Days 4–5 (Saratoga Springs to Plattsburgh):** ~25% canal towpath / bikeways, ~75% low-traffic scenic state highways & rolling country roads with wide paved shoulders along Lake Champlain.
  - **Day 6 (Plattsburgh into Montréal):** ~65% dedicated traffic-free bike paths (Chambly Canal National Historic Site Path, Route Verte #1 & #2, Samuel De Champlain Bridge multi-use path), ~35% quiet rural paved roads.
- **Recommended Bicycle:** Gravel bike, endurance road bike, or touring hybrid with 32mm–40mm puncture-resistant tires.
- **Luggage Strategy:** Credit-card touring style (hotel-to-hotel). Saddle pack, handlebar roll, and frame bag, or lightweight rear rack with waterproof micro-panniers (10–12 kg total luggage weight).

## ⚠️ Critical Tour Advisories & Operational Realities (Read Before Riding)

> [!CAUTION]
> ### 1. Amtrak Adirondack Train #68 Bike Slot Cap (Only 4–6 Bikes Per Train!)
> Amtrak’s *Adirondack* (Montreal Gare Centrale &rarr; New York Penn Station) provides roll-on bicycle service in the baggage car, but **strictly caps reservations at 4 to 6 bicycles per train**. 
> - **Action Required:** Book your Amtrak bicycle reservation ($20 add-on) **at the same time you plan your departure**. Once those slots sell out, bikes are prohibited on board (even boxed).
> - **Contingency Return Plans:**
>   1. **One-Way Rental Car:** Reserve a minivan or SUV through Enterprise or Avis at Montreal Gare Centrale or Montreal-Trudeau Airport (YUL) for one-way drop-off in NYC. (Verify cross-border rental permission).
>   2. **Trailways / Greyhound Bus:** Requires bikes to be boxed (pedals off, handlebars turned). Cardboard bike boxes can be purchased at *Bicycles Quilicot* (1775 Rue Saint-Denis, Montreal).
>   3. **Plattsburgh Rollback:** Ride 45 miles south back to Plattsburgh, NY, where US domestic car rental and bus options are plentiful and cheap.

> [!WARNING]
> ### 2. Foliage Season Lodging Spike & The Whitehall Reality
> - **Peak Foliage (Early October):** October 4–9 is peak autumn foliage. Rooms in Hudson, Saratoga Springs, and Lake Champlain spike to **$350–$500+/night** and sell out weeks in advance. Book early!
> - **Whitehall Overnight Reality:** Whitehall, NY (Day 4 on the 6-day tour) has **virtually zero chain hotel rooms**. The official stage finish is at the **Best Western Plus Ticonderoga** (Mile 61.8). If stopping early in Whitehall, stay at local Airbnbs/B&Bs or detour 10 flat miles east to the *Fair Haven Inn* in Fair Haven, VT.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> ### 3. October Daylight & "October Grease" Rules
> - **Sunset is at 6:25 PM:** Civil twilight ends by 6:52 PM, and temperatures drop 15°F the moment the sun dips behind the Adirondacks.
> - **Strict 8:30 AM Wheels-Rolling Rule:** Groups must start riding by 8:30 AM every day to ensure safe arrival by 3:00–4:30 PM before cold dusk sets in.
> - **"October Grease" on Rail Trails:** Wet, decaying autumn leaves on paved rail trails (Putnam, Maybrook, AHET) have the slickness of wet ice. Run **35mm–40mm tires at 45–50 PSI** and scrub speed before cornering.
> - **Lighting Mandate:** Minimum **800+ lumen headlight** and daylight-visible rear strobe (cold temperatures drain lithium batteries 30% faster; carry a 10,000+ mAh power bank).

> [!CAUTION]
> ### 4. Canadian Border Crossing Protocols (Lacolle / Route 221)
> - **Strict Zero-Cannabis Law:** While cannabis is legal in both New York and Quebec, **transporting any cannabis or THC product across the international border in either direction is a federal crime / felony**. Discard all THC products before departing NYC.
> - **Bike Serial Number & Proof of Purchase:** US CBP can demand proof of US origin upon return. **Photograph your bike's serial number** (under the bottom bracket) and carry a digital copy of your purchase receipt or home insurance policy on your phone.
> - **Agricultural Limits:** Fresh apples, citrus, and raw meats are confiscated at the Canadian border. Consume farm stand snacks before reaching Rouses Point.
> - **Offline Maps:** Cell coverage drops 3 miles before the Canadian border. Download offline Google Maps for the Albany-to-Montreal region in advance.

> [!NOTE]
> ### 5. Rural Bike Shop Blackout & Champlain Food Deserts
> - **Sunday/Monday Closures:** Rural bike shops in upstate NY are **closed on Sundays and Mondays**. You must be mechanically self-sufficient (carry 2x spare tubes, tubeless plug kit, chain quick-link, mini pump).
> - **Lake Champlain Supply Desert:** There is a 25-mile stretch between Port Henry and Keeseville on Day 5 with zero open convenience stores. **Fill both 24oz water bottles** in Port Henry and Essex!

## Detailed Daily Stages

### Sunday • Day 1: New York City to Brewster / Carmel, NY
*The Weekend Escape: Manhattan to Westchester & Putnam Greenways*

- **Distance:** 54.2 miles (87.2 km)
- **Climbing:** +1,850 ft / -1,320 ft
- **Route Segments:**
  - Depart Battery Park or Columbus Circle (Central Park).
  - Hudson River Greenway north past the George Washington Bridge to Dyckman St / Broadway.
  - Van Cortlandt Park (Bronx) connection to South County Trailway.
  - South County Trailway &rarr; North County Trailway through Westchester County.
  - Putnam County Trailway through Mahopac directly into Brewster.
- **Surface:** 98% paved, smooth dedicated multi-use trail. Completely car-free once in Westchester.
- **Recommended Mid-Ride Stops:**
  - **Coffee Stop (Mile 24):** *The Roaster Cafe* (Elmsford) or *The Black Cow Coffee Co.* (Pleasantville).
  - **Lunch Stop (Mile 37):** **Trailside Cafe** in Yorktown Heights (1807 Commerce St). Located directly on the rail trail! Fresh fruit smoothies, paninis, wraps, outdoor tables.
  - **Afternoon Snack (Mile 46):** *Mahopac Village Deli* or *The Daily Scoop* ice cream in Mahopac.
- **Overnight Hotel Options:**
  - **Option A:** *Heidi's Inn* (1270 Route 22, Brewster, NY – Phone: 845-279-8011). Classic cyclist-friendly motel with ground-level bike access.
  - **Option B:** *Comfort Inn Brewster* (7-11 Peach Lake Rd, Brewster, NY – Phone: 845-483-7848). Modern rooms, indoor pool, hot breakfast included.
- **Bike Shops Along Sunday Route:**
  - *Yorktown Cycles:* 1899 Commerce St, Yorktown Heights, NY (Phone: 914-245-5504). Directly adjacent to the trail & lunch stop. Open Sundays for tubes, CO2, emergency adjustments.
  - *Brewster Bicycle:* 870 Route 22, Brewster, NY (Phone: 845-278-8422). End-of-day repair resource.
- **Transit Bailout Contingency:**
  - Metro-North Harlem Line runs directly parallel to the trail all day.
  - Bailout stations: Pleasantville, Chappaqua, Mount Kisco, Katonah, Goldens Bridge, Purdys, Croton Falls, and Brewster. Standard bikes allowed on Sunday off-peak trains.

### Monday • Day 2: Brewster to Hudson, NY
*Maybrook Trailway, Walkway Over the Hudson & Historic Warren Street*

- **Distance:** 64.5 miles (103.8 km)
- **Climbing:** +2,350 ft / -2,580 ft
- **Route Segments:**
  - Maybrook Trailway west from Brewster to Hopewell Junction (gorgeous protected rail trail crossing wetlands and reservoirs).
  - Dutchess Rail Trail northwest to Poughkeepsie.
  - **Walkway Over the Hudson:** Spectacular 1.28-mile converted rail viaduct, 212 feet above the Hudson River!
  - Hudson Valley Rail Trail to Highland, then north through Rhinebeck, Red Hook, Clermont, and Germantown.
  - Roll into the historic city of Hudson along the Hudson River escarpment.
- **Surface:** 70% paved rail trail, 30% scenic low-traffic secondary roads (Route 9G / River Rd).
- **Recommended Mid-Ride Stops:**
  - **Morning Coffee (Mile 21):** *Dutchess Coffee Company* (Hopewell Junction depot).
  - **Lunch Stop (Mile 37):** **Underground Coffee & Ales** in Highland (immediately after crossing the Walkway Over the Hudson) for gourmet pressed sandwiches, salads, espresso, and craft beers.
  - **Afternoon Break (Mile 47):** *Bread Alone Bakery* in Rhinebeck or *Village Diner* in Red Hook.
- **Overnight Hotel Options in Hudson:**
  - **Option A (Boutique Main Street):** *The Barlow Hotel* (542 Warren St, Hudson, NY – Phone: 518-828-2100). Elegant boutique rooms with secure bike storage.
  - **Option B (Luxury Marriott Tribute):** *The Wick, Hudson* (41 Cross St, Hudson, NY – Phone: 518-249-6825). Modern luxury 1 block from Hudson Amtrak station.
  - **Option C (Nautical Historic):** *The Hudson Whaler Hotel* (542 Warren St, Hudson, NY – Phone: 518-217-4334).
- **Bike Shops Along Monday Route:**
  - *Bikeway - Hopewell Junction:* 812 Route 82, Hopewell Junction, NY (Phone: 845-223-8100). Direct access to rail trail at Mile 21.
  - *Rhinebeck Bicycle Shop:* 57 E Market St, Rhinebeck, NY (Phone: 845-876-4025). High-end repairs at Mile 47.
  - *Steiner's Sports - Hudson:* 301 Warren St, Hudson, NY (Phone: 518-828-5063). Directly in town for end-of-day maintenance.
- **Transit Bailout Contingency:**
  - Metro-North Hudson Line: Poughkeepsie Station (Mile 37).
  - Amtrak: Rhinecliff Station (Mile 47) and Hudson Station (Mile 64.5).

### Tuesday • Day 3: Hudson to Saratoga Springs, NY
*Albany-Hudson Electric Trail, Troy Waterfront & Saratoga Spa Gateway*

- **Distance:** 65.2 miles (104.9 km)
- **Climbing:** +1,850 ft / -1,720 ft
- **Route Segments:**
  - Pick up the **Albany-Hudson Electric Trail (AHET)** north out of Hudson/Kinderhook: 36 miles of smooth off-road paved and stone dust trail through Valatie, Nassau, and East Greenbush.
  - Cross into Rensselaer and across the river into Downtown Albany or Troy.
  - Follow the Mohawk-Hudson Bike Trail north to Peebles Island & Waterford (Lock 2 of Champlain Canal).
  - Pick up the paved **Zim Smith Trail** through Round Lake and Ballston Spa directly into historic Saratoga Springs.
- **Surface:** 65% paved multi-use rail trail (AHET & Zim Smith), 35% quiet canal roads & marked bike lanes.
- **Recommended Mid-Ride Stops:**
  - **Morning Snack (Mile 16):** *Broad Street Bagel Co.* or *Golden Harvest Farms Cider Donuts* (Valatie/Kinderhook).
  - **Lunch Stop (Mile 38):** **Downtown Troy Waterfront** – **The Placid Baker** (artisan sandwiches & pastries) or **The Whistling Kettle** (quiches, paninis, and specialty teas), or continue 5 miles to **Waterford Harbor Visitor Center** at Canal Lock 2.
  - **Afternoon Refreshment (Mile 56):** *The Mill on Round Lake* along the Zim Smith Trail.
- **Overnight Hotel Options in Saratoga Springs:**
  - **Option A (Heart of Broadway):** *The Saratoga Hilton* (534 Broadway, Saratoga Springs, NY – Phone: 518-584-4000). Central downtown location, walking distance to natural mineral springs.
  - **Option B (Cyclist Favorite):** *Holiday Inn Saratoga Springs* (232 Broadway, Saratoga Springs, NY – Phone: 518-584-4550). Very bike-friendly, indoor heated pool.
  - **Option C (Spacious Suites):** *Embassy Suites by Hilton Saratoga Springs* (86 Congress St – Phone: 518-290-9090). Hot cooked breakfast included.
- **Bike Shops Along Tuesday Route:**
  - *Steiner's Sports - Valatie:* 3455 US-9, Valatie, NY (Phone: 518-784-3663). Directly off the AHET trail at Mile 16.
  - *Down Tube Bicycle Works:* 466 Madison Ave, Albany, NY (Phone: 518-434-1711).
  - *Saratoga Bicycle Specialties:* 110 Excelsior Ave, Saratoga Springs, NY (Phone: 518-587-0071). Full-service road & gravel shop.
- **Transit Bailout Contingency:**
  - Amtrak Albany-Rensselaer Station (Mile 36) and Amtrak Saratoga Springs Station (Mile 65).

### Wednesday • Day 4: Saratoga Springs to Ticonderoga / Port Henry, NY
*Champlain Canalway, Birthplace of the US Navy & Fort Ticonderoga*

- **Distance:** 61.8 miles (99.5 km)
- **Climbing:** +2,150 ft / -2,320 ft
- **Route Segments:**
  - Saratoga Springs east to Schuylerville along the Saratoga Battlefield corridor.
  - Pick up the Champlain Canalway corridor north through Fort Edward, Fort Ann, and into **Whitehall, NY** (Birthplace of the U.S. Navy; Lock 12 of Champlain Canal).
  - Head north on NY-22 through the rolling hills flanking the South Bay of Lake Champlain.
  - Pass the historic ramparts of **Fort Ticonderoga** into the town of Ticonderoga (or continue to Port Henry).
- **Surface:** 75% paved rural state highways (Route 4 & NY-22) with moderate rolling hills and good paved shoulders; 25% canal trail / towpath.
- **Recommended Mid-Ride Stops:**
  - **Morning Coffee (Mile 12):** *Bound by Fate Brewing / Cafe* in Schuylerville.
  - **Lunch Stop (Mile 34):** **Whitehall, NY** – **Historic Grounds Restaurant & Coffee Bar** (142 Main St) for hearty paninis, hot soups, and espresso overlooking Lock 12 Park.
  - **Afternoon Treat (Mile 58):** *WindChill Factory* (Ticonderoga) – Famous gourmet soft-serve ice cream and cold drinks.
- **Overnight Hotel Options:**
  - **Option A (Ticonderoga Center):** *Best Western Plus Ticonderoga Inn & Suites* (260 Burgoyne Rd, Ticonderoga, NY – Phone: 518-585-1800). Large rooms, indoor pool, hot breakfast, extremely cyclist accommodating.
  - **Option B (Port Henry / Moriah):** *The Stone House B&B* (Port Henry, NY – Phone: 518-546-3400). Historic Victorian bed and breakfast overlooking Lake Champlain.
- **Bike Shops & Equipment Along Wednesday Route:**
  - *Rick's Bike Shop:* 368 Dix Ave, Queensbury / Glens Falls (Phone: 518-793-8986). Massive parts inventory (10 mi west of Fort Edward).
  - *Walker's Hardware & Supply:* 5565 NY-4, Fort Ann, NY (Phone: 518-639-5223). Emergency hardware, pumps, tubes.
  - *Ticonderoga Auto & Hardware:* Basic tools, tire repair, lubricants.
- **Transit Bailout Contingency:**
  - Amtrak Adirondack / Ethan Allen: Fort Edward Station (Mile 19), Whitehall Station (Mile 34), and Ticonderoga Station (Mile 60).

### Thursday • Day 5: Ticonderoga / Port Henry to Plattsburgh, NY
*Adirondack Coastline: Historic Essex, Ausable Chasm & Lake Vistas*

- **Distance:** 55.2 miles (88.8 km)
- **Climbing:** +1,950 ft / -1,980 ft
- **Route Segments:**
  - North from Port Henry on NY-9N/NY-22 along the dramatic bluffs overlooking Lake Champlain.
  - Pass through picturesque lakeside hamlets of **Westport** and **Essex** (one of the most intact 19th-century villages in America, with ferry service to Vermont).
  - Continue north through Willsboro, crossing the Boquet River.
  - Ride through Keeseville near **Ausable Chasm** ("Grand Canyon of the Adirondacks").
  - Glide down through Peru along the Lake Champlain shoreline on Route 9 into Plattsburgh.
- **Surface:** 80% scenic two-lane paved highway (NY-9N / NY-22) with panoramic lake views and good shoulders; 20% local paved paths and low-speed town roads.
- **Recommended Mid-Ride Stops:**
  - **Morning Coffee (Mile 15):** *Westport Marina Cafe* or *Ledge Hill Brewing* in Westport.
  - **Lunch Stop (Mile 27):** **Historic Essex, NY** – **The Cup Cafe** or **Essex Ice Cream Cafe** (*Chez Lin & Rays*). Sit on the lakeside porch and watch the ferry boats cross to Charlotte, Vermont.
  - **Afternoon Snack (Mile 43):** *Ausable Brewing Company* (Keeseville) – Rustic pavilion, craft ginger sodas, and farm snacks.
- **Overnight Hotel Options in Plattsburgh:**
  - **Option A:** *Best Western Plus Plattsburgh* (446 NY-3, Plattsburgh, NY – Phone: 518-561-7000). Full amenities, bike storage, complimentary hearty breakfast.
  - **Option B:** *Comfort Inn & Suites Plattsburgh* (411 Route 3, Plattsburgh, NY – Phone: 518-562-2730). Spacious suites, fitness center, indoor pool.
- **Bike Shops Along Thursday Route:**
  - *Maui North Ski, Bike & Board:* 168 Margaret St, Plattsburgh, NY (Phone: 518-563-7245). Top-tier downtown bike shop with expert mechanics, tubes, tires, and tune-ups.
  - *Viking Cindicat Bicycles:* 14 Skyway Shopping Center, Plattsburgh, NY (Phone: 518-561-5500).
- **Transit Bailout Contingency:**
  - Amtrak Adirondack: Westport Station (Mile 15), Port Kent Station (seasonal), and Plattsburgh Station (Mile 55).

### Friday • Day 6: Plattsburgh, NY to Montréal, QC (Canada)
*Arrival Day! International Border, Chambly Canal & Old Montréal Finish*

- **Distance:** 62.4 miles (100.4 km)
- **Climbing:** +850 ft / -950 ft (mostly flat with slight downhill trend to St. Lawrence River)
- **Route Segments:**
  - Plattsburgh north on US Route 9 through Chazy to **Rouses Point, NY** (Mile 24).
  - **U.S. / Canada International Border Crossing:** Cross at Rouses Point / Saint-Bernard-de-Lacolle (Route 221 / QC-223).
  - Enter Québec and join **Route Verte #1 & #2** heading north along the Richelieu River.
  - Arrive in **Saint-Jean-sur-Richelieu** (Mile 42).
  - Join the **Chambly Canal National Historic Site Path**: 20 km (12.5 miles) of completely flat, car-free crushed stone and paved pathway alongside the historic canal locks and yachts!
  - Reach historic Fort Chambly, then follow the paved Montée du Chemin de Chambly / Route Verte path through Longueuil / Brossard.
  - **The Grand Entrance into Montréal:** Cross the St. Lawrence River via the dedicated multi-use pathway of the **Samuel De Champlain Bridge** into Île-des-Sœurs, connecting directly to the Lachine Canal bike path and into the heart of Old Montréal (Vieux-Montréal) and Downtown!
- **Surface:** 65% dedicated traffic-free bike paths (Chambly Canal & Route Verte), 35% quiet rural paved roads.
- **Recommended Mid-Ride Stops:**
  - **Pre-Border Coffee (Mile 23):** *Lakeside Coffee* in Rouses Point.
  - **Lunch Stop (Mile 42):** **Saint-Jean-sur-Richelieu, QC** – **Bistro La Trinquette** (155 Rue Richelieu). Riverside patio dining right at the entrance of the Chambly Canal. Authentic Quebec fare, paninis, crepes, cider.
  - **Afternoon Victory Toast (Mile 52):** **Chambly, QC** – **Microbrasserie Délires et Délices** or *Restaurant Fourquet Fourchette* overlooking the rapids of Fort Chambly.
- **Overnight Hotel Options in Montréal:**
  - **Option A (Certified "Bienvenue Cyclistes"):** *Hotel Monville* (1041 Rue de Bleury, Montréal – Phone: +1 514-379-2000). Ultra-modern, secure locked bicycle room, steps from Place d'Armes.
  - **Option B (Boutique Old Montreal):** *Le Petit Hotel* (168 Rue Saint-Paul O, Montréal – Phone: +1 514-985-0090). Romantic boutique hotel in the historic cobblestone quarter.
  - **Option C (Directly above Amtrak):** *Fairmont The Queen Elizabeth* (900 René-Lévesque Blvd W – Phone: +1 514-861-3511). Directly connected to Gare Centrale for effortless Amtrak roll-on return!
- **Bike Shops in Saint-Jean, Chambly & Montréal:**
  - *Performance Bégin:* 470 Rue Saint-Jacques, Saint-Jean-sur-Richelieu, QC (Phone: 450-348-3561).
  - *Vélo Chambly:* 1740 Ave Bourgogne, Chambly, QC (Phone: 450-447-8356).
  - *Bicycles Quilicot:* 1775 Rue Saint-Denis, Montréal, QC (Phone: 514-499-9600).
- **Return Travel & Contingency:**
  - **Amtrak Adirondack Train #68:** Operates daily from Montréal Gare Centrale back to New York Penn Station with roll-on bicycle service ($20 reservation fee; no box required).

## Contingency Plans & Border Crossing Protocol

### 1. Rail Bailout Safety Net (Amtrak & Metro-North)
- **Sunday & Monday (NYC to Hudson):** Metro-North Harlem and Hudson lines operate frequent trains. Standard bicycles are permitted without reservation outside rush hours.
- **Monday & Tuesday (Hudson to Saratoga):** Amtrak Empire Service connects Hudson and Albany-Rensselaer.
- **Wednesday to Friday (Saratoga to Montréal):** **Amtrak Adirondack (Train 68/69)** shadows the route every day! Stops include Fort Edward, Whitehall, Ticonderoga, Port Henry, Plattsburgh, and Montréal Gare Centrale. Roll-on bike service is available.

### 2. Friday Border Crossing Protocol (Canada Border Services Agency)
- **Crossing:** Rouses Point, NY / Saint-Bernard-de-Lacolle, QC (Route 221 / QC-223).
- **Required Documents:** Valid Passport (at least 6 months validity) or Enhanced Driver's License.
- **Cyclist Etiquette:** Stop behind the white line at the primary vehicle inspection booth; remove helmet and sunglasses when speaking to the CBSA officer.
- **Prohibited Items:** Bear spray / pepper spray (strictly prohibited felony import into Canada), cannabis/THC products (strictly illegal across international borders), and fresh fruits/produce.
